FOUNDATIONS / 01

PRINCIPLES

OFF-REGISTER ON PURPOSE

Color channels sit a few pixels off, like a cheap two-pass print. The slip is the signature — controlled imperfection that signals hand-made, underground, real.

SPOT INKS, NOT GRADIENTS

Three flat fluoro inks do the work. No soft gradients, no glow — color is laid down solid and lets the halftone do the mixing, the way a risograph does.

GRAIN IS THE GLUE

A faint, living film grain sits over everything and ties the page together. It moves — never a static stamp — so the surface feels like running film, warm and analog.

CLEAN BONES, LOUD SKIN

The texture is maximal; the structure is not. A strict grid, mono labels and clear hierarchy keep the work navigable. Wild surface, disciplined skeleton.
